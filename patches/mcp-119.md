# Creature voiceover enable

Patch key: `mcp-119`

Creatures can use recorded Hello, Idle, and Attack voice lines when a mod provides dialogue and audio for them. The retail engine restricts those voice paths to NPCs.

## How it works

The patch lets creatures through the combat start and attack animation voice gates, and through the greeting check. The normal dialogue conditions and voice file lookup still decide whether a line plays. Hello lines require a positive AI Hello rating and a package that permits greetings.

See the [patch table](../docs/patches.md) for availability and selection.
