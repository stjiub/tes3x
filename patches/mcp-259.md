# Accurate item weight display

Patch key: `mcp-259`

Item tooltips show weight to two decimal places. This makes light items easier to compare when
their weights would round to the same tenth.

## How it works

The patch changes the weight field in the engine's item tooltip format from one decimal place to
two. It leaves the other numbers in the tooltip at their original precision. See the
[patch table](../docs/patches.md) for availability and selection.
