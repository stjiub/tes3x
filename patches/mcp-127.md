# World map texture independence

Patch key: `mcp-127`

Replacement land textures can have different dimensions from the retail texture. The world map
then appears blank or cropped because its panel size follows that texture. This patch lets texture
mods change the land texture without changing the world map layout.

## How it works

The map panel loads the fixed `menu_icon_none.tga` texture for its sizing reference and adjusts
its scale to preserve the retail panel size. The land texture remains available for the map itself.
See the [patch table](../docs/patches.md) for availability and selection.
