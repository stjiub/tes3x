# Nocked arrow removal

While a bow or crossbow is fully drawn, retail offers no way to put the arrow back: the shot must
be released. With this patch, pressing Ready Weapon removes the nocked projectile and leaves the
weapon readied. The ammunition remains in the inventory because the engine has not fired or
consumed it yet.

See the [patch table](../docs/patches.md) for availability and selection.

## How it works

The Xbox input loop checks `MobileActor::isAttackingOrCasting` before its Ready Weapon handler and
normally skips all action handling while a shot is held. The patch redirects that check to a shim
(`hooks/tes3xmcp146.c`). When action 6 is pressed, the shim performs MCP's de-nock operation on
the temporary projectile at `MobilePlayer+0x100`, marks the input consumed, and resumes after the
normal weapon-toggle path.
