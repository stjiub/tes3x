# Arrow de-nocker

While a bow or crossbow is fully drawn, pressing Ready Weapon destroys the temporary nocked
projectile and leaves the weapon readied. The ammunition remains in the inventory because the
engine has not fired or consumed it yet.

The Xbox input loop checks `MobileActor::isAttackingOrCasting` before its Ready Weapon handler and
normally skips all action handling while a shot is held. The patch redirects that check to a shim.
When action 6 is pressed, the shim performs MCP's de-nock operation on the temporary projectile at
`MobilePlayer+0x100`, marks the input consumed, and resumes after the normal weapon-toggle path.
