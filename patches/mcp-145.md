# Quality-based potion appearance

Patch key: `mcp-145`

Brewed potions use a bottle model and icon that reflect their strength instead of a random one.
The five appearances progress from Bargain through Cheap, Standard and Quality to Exclusive.
This makes the result of alchemy easier to recognize in the inventory and world.

## How it works

The patch uses the potion's base strength, which the engine also uses to calculate its value.
Strengths below 15, 30, 45 and 60 mark the first four tiers; 60 and above uses Exclusive.
The appearance does not change the potion's effects, value or weight. Merchant potions keep
their authored appearance.
